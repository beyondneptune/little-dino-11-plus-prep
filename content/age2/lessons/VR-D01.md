# Number sequences
*Hyneria station · Age 2: Devonian Seas*

## Learn
In a number sequence, the numbers follow a **rule**. Find the **step** from one
number to the next (how much it goes up or down), then keep the step going to find
the missing number.

## Worked examples
1. **3, 6, 9, 12, ?** The step is 3 each time: 12 + 3 = **15**.
2. **20, 17, 14, 11, ?** The step is take away 3: 11 − 3 = **8**.
   *Common mistake:* changing the step partway along.

## Remember
Find the step, then keep it going.
