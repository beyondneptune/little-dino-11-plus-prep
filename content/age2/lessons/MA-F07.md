# Perimeter
*Dunkleosteus station · Age 2: Devonian Seas*

## Learn
The **perimeter** is the distance **all the way round** a shape. To find it, add
up the length of every side. A rectangle has two long sides and two short sides.

## Worked examples
1. **A rectangle is 5 cm by 3 cm.** The long sides: 5 + 5 = **10**. The short
   sides: 3 + 3 = **6**. All the way round: 10 + 6 = **16** cm.
   *Common mistake:* adding only one long and one short side.
2. **A square has sides of 4 cm.** All four sides are equal: 4 + 4 = 8, and
   8 + 8 = **16** cm.

## Remember
Perimeter is the total of all the sides.
