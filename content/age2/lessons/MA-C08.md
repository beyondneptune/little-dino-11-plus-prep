# Fractions of amounts
*Dunkleosteus station · Age 2: Devonian Seas*

## Learn
To find a fraction of an amount, **divide by the bottom number** (how many equal
parts there are), then **times by the top number** (how many parts you want).

## Worked examples
1. **Find 3/4 of 20.** Divide by 4: 20 ÷ 4 = **5**. Then times by 3: 5 × 3 = **15**.
   *Common mistake:* timesing by 4 instead of dividing.
2. **Find 2/3 of 18.** Divide by 3: 18 ÷ 3 = **6**. Then times by 2: 6 × 2 = **12**.

## Remember
Divide by the bottom, then times by the top.
