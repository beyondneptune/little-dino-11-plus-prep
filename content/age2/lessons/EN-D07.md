# Sentence grammar
*Eusthenopteron station · Age 2: Devonian Seas*

## Learn
The **verb** must match the person doing it. For **one** person or thing we often
add an **s**: "The dog **runs**." For **more than one**, we do not: "The dogs
**run**." Watch out for "is" and "are": use **is** for one ("The child is happy")
and **are** for more than one ("The children are happy").

## Remember
One thing, add s (runs). More than one, no s (run). One = is, many = are.
