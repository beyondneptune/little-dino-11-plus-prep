# Money and change
*Dunkleosteus station · Age 2: Devonian Seas*

## Learn
**Change** is the money you get back. To find it, take the **cost** away from the
**amount you paid**. Line up the pounds and the pennies before you subtract.

## Worked examples
1. **You pay £5 and the cost is £3.** Your change is 5 − 3 = **£2**.
2. **You pay £10 and the cost is £6.** Your change is 10 − 6 = **£4**.
   *Common mistake:* adding the cost on instead of taking it away.

## Remember
Change = what you paid − what it cost.
