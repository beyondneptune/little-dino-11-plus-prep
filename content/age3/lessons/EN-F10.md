# Plurals
*Pulmonoscorpius station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
A **plural** means **more than one**. For most words we add **s**: one cat, two
**cats**. For words ending in s, x, ch or sh, we add **es**: one box, two
**boxes**. For words ending in a **y** after a letter that is not a vowel, we
change the y to **ies**: one baby, two **babies**. Some words are special, like
one child, two **children**.

## Remember
Add s for most, es after s/x/ch/sh, and change y to ies.
