# Apostrophes in contractions
*Hylonomus station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
A **contraction** is a short form of two words joined together. We use an
**apostrophe** to show where letters are missing. "do not" becomes **don't** (the
apostrophe stands for the missing **o**). "it is" becomes **it's**. "they are"
becomes **they're**. The apostrophe always goes exactly where the letters were
taken out.

## Remember
An apostrophe shows missing letters: do not becomes don't.
