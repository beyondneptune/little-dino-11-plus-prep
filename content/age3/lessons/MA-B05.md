# Order of operations
*Meganeura station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
When a calculation has more than one step, do them in the right order:
**B**rackets first, then **D**ivide and **M**ultiply, then **A**dd and
**S**ubtract. This is called BODMAS.

## Worked examples
1. **Work out 4 × 5 + 3.** Do the times first: 4 × 5 = 20. Then add: 20 + 3 = **23**.
   *Common mistake:* adding 5 + 3 first.
2. **Work out 6 + 2 × 4.** Do the times first: 2 × 4 = 8. Then add: 6 + 8 = **14**.

## Remember
Divide and multiply before you add and subtract.
