# Prime numbers
*Meganeura station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
A **prime number** has exactly **two** factors: 1 and itself. It cannot be made by
timesing two smaller numbers (other than 1). The first primes are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11.

## Worked examples
1. **Is 7 prime?** Yes — nothing times together to make 7 except 1 × 7. It is prime.
2. **Is 9 prime?** No — 3 × 3 = 9, so 9 has a factor of 3. It is **not** prime.
   *Common mistake:* thinking every odd number is prime.

## Remember
A prime has only two factors: 1 and itself. (1 is not a prime.)
