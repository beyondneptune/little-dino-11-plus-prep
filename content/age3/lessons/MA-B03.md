# Long multiplication
*Meganeura station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
To multiply a bigger number, split it into **tens and ones**, multiply each part,
then add the parts back together.

## Worked examples
1. **Work out 24 × 4.** The ones: 4 × 4 = 16. The tens: 20 × 4 = 80.
   Add them: 80 + 16 = **96**.
   *Common mistake:* forgetting to add the two parts together.
2. **Work out 13 × 3.** The ones: 3 × 3 = 9. The tens: 10 × 3 = 30.
   Add them: 30 + 9 = **39**.

## Remember
Split into tens and ones, multiply each, then add.
