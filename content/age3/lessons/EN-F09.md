# Homophones
*Pulmonoscorpius station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
**Homophones** are words that **sound the same** but are spelled differently and
mean different things. "there" is a place ("over there"), "their" means belonging
to them ("their coats"), and "they're" is short for "they are". Read the whole
sentence and think about the **meaning** to choose the right one.

## Remember
Homophones sound the same but mean different things. Use the meaning to pick the
right spelling.
