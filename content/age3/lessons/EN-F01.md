# Spelling
*Hylonomus station · Age 3: Carboniferous Swamps*

## Learn
Some words are tricky to spell. It helps to **look carefully** at each letter and
learn the parts that are easy to get wrong. For example, **because** has the
letters c-a-u-s-e. **Friend** has the word **end** in it, with an **i** before the
**e**: fr-i-end. When you are not sure, break the word into smaller parts and say
each part slowly.

## Remember
Look carefully at the tricky part of each word and learn it bit by bit.
