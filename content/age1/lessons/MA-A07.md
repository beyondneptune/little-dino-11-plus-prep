# Roman numerals
*Trilobite station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
Roman numerals use letters for numbers: **I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50,
C = 100, D = 500, M = 1000**. You usually **add** the letters from left to right.
But when a **smaller** letter comes **before** a bigger one, you **subtract** it.
So IV is 5 − 1 = 4, and IX is 10 − 1 = 9.

## Worked examples
1. **What is XV?** X is 10 and V is 5. Both go from big to small, so add:
   10 + 5 = **15**.
2. **What is XIV?** X is 10. Then IV is 4 (because I is before V). So
   10 + 4 = **14**.
   *Common mistake:* adding every letter to get sixteen and forgetting to
   subtract the I that sits in front of the V.
3. **What is XL?** X (10) is before L (50), so subtract: 50 − 10 = **40**.

## Remember
Add the letters, but subtract a smaller letter that sits in front of a bigger one.
