# Mental adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing
*Trilobite station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
This is about doing **one** calculation in your head quickly and accurately —
a single **add, take-away, times or divide**. Know your number bonds and times
tables, and divides become easy because they are just times tables backwards.

## Worked examples
1. **Work out 48 + 35.** Add the tens (40 + 30 = 70), then the ones (8 + 5 = 13),
   then put them together: 70 + 13 = **83**.
   *Common mistake:* forgetting to carry the extra ten from 8 + 5.
2. **Work out 7 × 8.** This is a times-table fact: 7 × 8 = **56**.
   *Common mistake:* mixing it up with 7 × 7 = 49.
3. **Work out 54 ÷ 6.** A divide is a times table backwards: 6 × 9 = 54, so
   54 ÷ 6 = **9**.
   *Common mistake:* 6 × 8 = 48 is too small — keep going.

## Going further — more than one step (BODMAS)
Harder 11+ questions join steps together. Do them in the right order:
**B**rackets first, then **D**ivide and **M**ultiply, then **A**dd and
**S**ubtract. So in 7 × 8 − 15, do the times first: 56 − 15 = **41**. In
9 × 6 + 12 ÷ 4, do × and ÷ first: 54 + 3 = **57**.
*Common mistake:* working strictly left to right and getting the order wrong.

## Remember
One step, done carefully. For longer questions, use BODMAS: brackets, then
÷ and ×, then + and −.
