# Prefixes and suffixes
*Hallucigenia station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
A **prefix** is a small part added to the **start** of a word to change its
meaning. "un" means *not*, so "un" + "kind" makes **unkind**. "re" means *again*,
so "re" + "do" makes **redo**. A **suffix** is added to the **end** of a word.
"ful" means *full of*, so "help" + "ful" makes **helpful**. "less" means
*without*, so "care" + "less" makes **careless**.

## Remember
A prefix goes at the start and a suffix goes at the end. Think about what each part
means.
