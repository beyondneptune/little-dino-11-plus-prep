# Odd, even and multiples
*Anomalocaris station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
An **even** number can be split into two equal groups. Even numbers end in
**0, 2, 4, 6 or 8**. An **odd** number has one left over; odd numbers end in
**1, 3, 5, 7 or 9**. A **multiple** of a number is what you get in its times
table — a multiple of 5 is any number you reach by counting in fives.

## Worked examples
1. **Is 34 odd or even?** It ends in 4, so it is **even**.
2. **Is 45 a multiple of 5?** Yes, because 9 × 5 = **45**. Numbers in the 5 times
   table end in **0 or 5**.
   *Common mistake:* thinking 52 is a multiple of 5 — it ends in 2, so it is not.
3. **Is 90 a multiple of 10?** Yes, because 9 × 10 = **90**, and multiples of 10
   end in **0**.

## Remember
Even ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. A multiple is a number from that times table.
