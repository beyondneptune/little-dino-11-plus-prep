# Rounding to the nearest 10 or 100
*Trilobite station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
To round, look at the **next digit along**. If it is **5 or more, round up**.
If it is **4 or less, round down** (the number stays the same size, the smaller
digits become 0).

## Worked examples
1. **Round 2321 to the nearest 10.** Look at the ones digit: 1. That is less than
   5, so round **down** → **2320**.
   *Common mistake:* changing the tens digit when you should leave it.
2. **Round 6473 to the nearest 100.** Look at the tens digit: 7. That is 5 or more,
   so round **up** → **6500**.
   *Common mistake:* picking 6400 by rounding down.
3. **Round 450 to the nearest 100.** The tens digit is 5, so round **up** → **500**.

## Remember
Look at the next digit: 5 or more rounds up, 4 or less rounds down.
