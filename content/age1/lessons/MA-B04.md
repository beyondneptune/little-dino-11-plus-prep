# Times tables and division facts
*Anomalocaris station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
Division undoes multiplication. **54 ÷ 9** is really asking: *9 times what makes
54?* If you know your times tables, you already know the answer.

## Worked examples
1. **54 ÷ 9.** Think: 9 × 6 = 54. So 54 ÷ 9 = **6**.
   *Common mistake:* 9 × 5 = 45 is too small — keep going.
2. **336 ÷ 4.** Think: 4 × 84 = 336. So 336 ÷ 4 = **84**.
   You can split it: 4 × 80 = 320, and 4 × 4 = 16, so 4 × 84 = 336.
3. **72 ÷ 8.** 8 × 9 = 72, so 72 ÷ 8 = **9**.

## Remember
For a divide, find the times-table fact that matches.
