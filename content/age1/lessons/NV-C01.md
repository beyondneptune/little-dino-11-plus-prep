# Odd one out
*Wiwaxia station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
Four of the pictures share something. They might be the **same shape**, have the
**same shading**, have the **same number of dots**, or **point the same way**.
One picture is not like the others. Look at one thing at a time. The odd one is
the picture that is different.

## Remember
Find what most pictures share. The odd one is the one that does not fit.
