# Missing numbers
*Anomalocaris station · Age 1: Cambrian Seas*

## Learn
A missing number puzzle hides one number in a calculation. To find it, use the
**inverse** (opposite) operation. The opposite of **+ is −**, and the opposite of
**× is ÷**. Undo what was done to the box, and you find the number that fits.

## Worked examples
1. **7 × ? = 56.** The box was multiplied by 7, so do the opposite and divide:
   56 ÷ 7 = **8**. Check: 7 × 8 = 56. ✓
2. **23 + ? = 40.** The box was added to 23, so subtract: 40 − 23 = **17**.
   Check: 23 + 17 = 40. ✓
   *Common mistake:* adding 23 and 40 instead of subtracting.
3. **48 ÷ ? = 6.** We need a number that divides 48 into groups of 6:
   48 ÷ 6 = **8**. Check: 48 ÷ 8 = 6. ✓

## Remember
Undo the operation with its opposite, then check your answer fits.
